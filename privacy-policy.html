Privacy Policy - PixOptimizer

Last updated: August 6, 2026

PixOptimizer: Optimize Images & Convert is an application designed to help users optimize and convert image files quickly and easily.

This Privacy Policy explains how PixOptimizer handles information when you use the application.

Information We Collect

PixOptimizer is designed to process images locally on the user's device whenever possible.

The application does not require users to create an account and does not directly collect personal information such as:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Home address
  • Passwords
  • Contacts
  • Personal photos for storage on our servers

Images selected by the user are used only to perform the requested optimization or format conversion.

Image Processing

PixOptimizer allows users to:

  • Reduce the file size of images.
  • Optimize images while maintaining good visual quality.
  • Convert images between WEBP, PNG and JPG formats.
  • Select basic image quality options.

Images selected for optimization or conversion are processed for the purpose requested by the user.

PixOptimizer does not intentionally upload or store users' images on its own servers.

Advertising

PixOptimizer may display advertisements through third-party advertising services.

These advertising providers may collect certain information automatically, such as:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location
  • App interaction information
  • Advertising and diagnostic data

This information may be used to provide, measure and personalize advertisements, subject to the policies of the advertising provider.

Removing Advertisements

PixOptimizer may offer a one-time in-app purchase that allows users to permanently remove advertisements from the application.

The purchase is processed through the platform's official billing system, such as Google Play Billing.

PixOptimizer does not directly receive or store users' payment card information.

Third-Party Services

The application may use third-party services for advertising, analytics, crash reporting, or in-app purchases.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies.

Examples may include:

  • Google Play Services
  • Google AdMob
  • Google Play Billing

Users should review the privacy policies of these third-party services for additional information about how their data is handled.

Children's Privacy

PixOptimizer is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable law.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application, please contact us so that appropriate action can be taken.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information associated with the application.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Data Retention

PixOptimizer does not intentionally store users' personal information or images on its own servers.

Information collected by third-party services may be retained according to their respective policies and terms.

Your Choices

Depending on your device and applicable laws, you may have options to:

  • Control advertising personalization.
  • Reset your advertising identifier.
  • Limit certain data collection.
  • Manage application permissions.
  • Uninstall the application.

You can also purchase the ad-removal option if it is available in the application.

Changes to This Privacy Policy
